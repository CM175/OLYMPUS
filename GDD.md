# Game Design Document (GDD)
## Project Title: OLYMPUS

---

## 1. Game Overview

OLYMPUS is a team-based hero shooter set in Greek mythology, inspired by modern class-based shooters such as Overwatch and Marvel Rivals.

Players select mythological heroes, gods, and creatures and compete in objective-based multiplayer battles. Each character has unique abilities, weapons, and an ultimate ability tied to their mythological powers.

The game combines:
- Fast-paced shooter mechanics
- Strategic hero abilities
- Mythological storytelling
- Team-based gameplay

The game's unique identity comes from its Greek mythology setting, divine powers instead of traditional guns, and team synergy mechanics between gods and heroes.

---

## 2. Core Gameplay

### Match Format
- Matches are played between two teams of 5 players.
- Each player selects a hero character before the match.
- Teams must work together using roles, abilities, and positioning to complete objectives.
- Typical match length: **10–15 minutes**

### Player Perspective
The game uses a first-person perspective, placing the player directly behind the eyes of their chosen hero for immersive combat and ability use.

---

## 3. Player Roles

To encourage teamwork and balance gameplay, heroes are divided into four roles.

### Vanguard (Tank)
Frontline characters with high health and defensive abilities.

**Responsibilities:**
- Protect teammates
- Absorb damage
- Control space

**Example heroes:** Zeus, Ares, Minotaur

---

### Striker (Damage)
Primary damage dealers focused on eliminating enemies.

**Responsibilities:**
- Deal high damage
- Secure eliminations
- Pressure enemy backline

**Example heroes:** Achilles, Artemis, Perseus

---

### Mystic (Support)
Support characters who heal, shield, or enhance allies.

**Responsibilities:**
- Heal teammates
- Provide buffs
- Offer strategic advantages

**Example heroes:** Athena, Hecate, Apollo

---

### Trickster (Flanker)
Highly mobile characters who disrupt enemies.

**Responsibilities:**
- Attack from behind
- Distract enemies
- Eliminate vulnerable targets

**Example heroes:** Hermes, Odysseus

---

## 4. Characters

Each hero has:
- Primary attack
- 2–3 abilities
- Ultimate ability
- Passive trait

---

### Zeus – Vanguard

**Primary Weapon:** Lightning bolts

**Abilities:**
- Sky Charge *(Mobility)*
- Storm Shield *(temporary barrier)*

**Ultimate:** Wrath of Olympus
> A massive lightning storm strikes enemies in an area.

**Passive:** Static energy slowly damages nearby enemies.

---

### Achilles – Striker

**Primary Weapon:** Spear and shield

**Abilities:**
- Spear Throw
- Shield Bash
- Battle Cry *(temporary damage boost)*

**Ultimate:** Achilles' Fury
> Achilles becomes unstoppable for a short time.

**Passive:** Reduced damage from frontal attacks.

---

### Athena – Mystic

**Primary Weapon:** Spear of Wisdom

**Abilities:**
- Tactical Shield *(protect ally)*
- Blessing of Wisdom *(heal ability)*

**Ultimate:** Divine Strategy
> All teammates gain increased defense and damage.

**Passive:** Nearby allies gain increased cooldown recovery.

---

### Hermes – Trickster

**Primary Weapon:** Winged daggers

**Abilities:**
- Blink Dash
- Backstab

**Ultimate:** Messenger of the Gods
> Hermes gains extreme movement speed and invisibility.

**Passive:** Faster movement speed than other heroes.

## 6. Game Modes

### Capture the Temple
- Teams fight to control a sacred point.
- The team that holds the point longer wins.

### Escort the Relic
- One team escorts an ancient artifact across the map.
- The other team attempts to stop them.

### Sacred Zone Control
- Multiple control points must be captured.
- Teams earn points for holding zones.

---

## 7. Unique Mechanics

### God Synergy System

Certain heroes unlock special abilities when played together.

| Heroes | Synergy Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Zeus + Poseidon | Storm Tsunami | Massive lightning-powered tidal wave |
| Athena + Ares | War Strategy | Teamwide damage and defense buff |
| Apollo + Artemis | Twin Hunt | Precision damage boost for both heroes |

### Mythological Power System

Instead of traditional guns, heroes use:
- Spears
- Swords
- Bows
- Magic
- Divine powers
- Creature summons

This reinforces the mythological theme throughout all combat.
